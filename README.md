** Description of the project **:

CareerConnect is a web-based application built for both job seekers and recruter by centralizing job offers in a single page, allowing easier tracking and
application process for the applicant and better filtering and visibility for recruter.


** Problem the project is solving **:

There exist multiple job seeking application, which fragments the list of pending demands accross many platform for a user. This cause larger overhead and 
time loss in a market which sometimes require hundreds of applications to get a positive answer. Job seekers are also required to spend a larger amount of time
searching a veryfing an applicant to process each demands.

** Solution offered **:

Through CareerConnect, seekers will be able to centralize their demand in one plateform and upload their resume for faster application. Filtering will also streamline
their research process. AI service are also offered to accelerate the creation of cover letter to generate higher odds of results.
